A playful, private app for partners to log and share silly "moments" — quick one-tap logging, shared timelines, and streaks.

Full description (avoid clinical language):
Sync is a light, private app that helps couples and close friends keep each other updated with quick, playful "moments". Log a moment with one tap, see your partner's activity in near real time, and celebrate "sync" streaks when you both log close together. Share a weekly recap card for laughs and bragging rights.

- One-tap logging (no photos in MVP)
- Private 1:1 pairing via invite code
- Real-time shared feed with playful notifications
- Home-screen widget for quick logging and streak glance
- Weekly recap card for easy sharing

Privacy & safety:

- Minimal PII collected (anonymous auth UID, optional display name).
- Privacy policy included in app and Play Console.
